Forward, organised by DSCubed and RAID, is a 48-hour hackathon focused specifically on applying AI in a business setting. Participants will have the opportunity to meet other passionate students, form a team, receive mentoring from experienced mentors and develop a project to tackle real-world challenges relating to business and AI.  Teams of 2-5 people will work through one of 4 challenge tracks throughout the hackathon, and are given 48 hours to bring their projects to life. During this period, participants will have access to coworking spaces and experienced mentors for support.  Sponsored by ElevenLabs and Eleno, leading companies in developing AI solutions for businesses, the total prize pool is valued at $3000+, along with additional prizes including free ElevenLabs access. There are also potential internships and job opportunities available at Eleno,  Through this event, participants will be able to gain invaluable experience connecting and networking with likeminded people and industry professional judges, and have a finished product that they can be proud of.



# Track 1: Improve an Existing Business Capability

Take something a business already does and make it significantly better using AI.

Choose an existing business function, workflow, or process and build a technical solution that improves how it operates. The improvement could make it faster, cheaper, more accurate, more scalable, easier to use, or capable of producing better outcomes.

The focus is on **augmenting or transforming an existing capability**, rather than inventing an entirely new one.

**Examples:** An AI customer-support agent that can resolve complex requests end-to-end; an intelligent document-processing pipeline for invoices and contracts; an AI coding or QA system for a software team; a sales assistant that researches and qualifies leads; a multimodal inventory-management system; an internal knowledge agent that can search company information and take actions; an AI system that improves demand forecasting or scheduling.

**Think:** *“Businesses already do X. How can AI make X dramatically better?”*



# Track 2: Create a New Business Capability

Build something that allows a business to do something it previously could not realistically do.

Use AI to create a new product, service, workflow, interface, or organisational capability. This could come from new developments in agents, multimodal models, voice, computer vision, generative systems, autonomous workflows, or entirely new ways for humans and AI to interact.

The focus is on **unlocking new possibilities**, rather than simply optimising an existing process.

**Examples:** An AI agent that autonomously negotiates with suppliers; a voice-based system that lets customers dynamically create personalised products; an AI research team that continuously investigates new markets; a system that turns unstructured company data into interactive simulations; an autonomous service that operates on behalf of customers; a new AI-native product that would not have been practical without modern models.

**Think:** *“What can a business do now that simply wasn't possible before?”*



# Track 3: Solve a Business Problem

Start with a meaningful business problem and build the best technical solution you can.

Identify a concrete problem faced by businesses, employees, customers, or industries and use AI as part of the solution. Unlike the other tracks, you do not need to begin with an existing capability or a particular AI technology. **Start with the problem.**

The problem can be operational, financial, strategic, technical, customer-facing, industry-specific, or something businesses have historically struggled to solve.

**Examples:** Reducing food or inventory waste; detecting fraud or unusual transactions; helping small businesses understand cash flow; reducing employee scheduling conflicts; identifying supply-chain risks; making complex regulations easier to navigate; matching businesses with suitable suppliers; reducing customer churn; improving accessibility of business services.

**Think:** *“What is a real problem worth solving, and what can we build in 48 hours to attack it?”*
